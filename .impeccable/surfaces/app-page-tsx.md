Scope: `/` only, full-bleed (no app sidebar). Mode: Persuade. Audience: NASA Space Apps 2026 judges, a few minutes each. They must leave believing the evidence is real and the gap analysis works. ## Direction contract

THESIS: The landing page proves the gap, not the hype: a typed cabin condition is checked live against the 13 verified NASA records and the page shows where testing stops. Refuses the hero-headline-plus-stat-cards template.

OWN-WORLD: Night instrument slate, amber only for brand and the user's own value, provenance hues for data, diagonal hatch wherever evidence is absent. Registration corner ticks on containers, hairline plot grids, IBM Plex Sans 500 display, Plex Mono only for values and units.

STORY: Fire in orbit is different; NASA tested specific conditions; IGNIS shows which ones and where nothing was tested; each record was checked by a second person; try your scenario.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 6/12: H1, one paragraph, working scenario form (oxygen %, pressure kPa, gravity g) submitting to /scenario, secondary link to /explore. Right 6/12: code-drawn spherical microgravity flame on canvas inside an open orbit arc, three bobbing tiles with computed counts. Floating header bar above.

FORM: Pinned structure (user brief), position 1 of 1. seed: none (concept-seed not run: the user pinned the section structure, so no direction roll was made). Signature interaction: oxygen slider over every investigation's tested range, covered rows light, untested rows hatch, live "n of 13" count.
